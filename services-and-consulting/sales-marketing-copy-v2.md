## Copy rules observed (for future edits)

- Christian framing is load-bearing everywhere: stewardship, vocation, craft-as-service, legacy, "good, beautiful, and true." Lean into legacy (building things that last, that outlive us). Still avoid prosperity/get-rich language — legacy is about enduring worth, not wealth.
- We state plainly that we are Christians ourselves — not just that we serve Christians. Our faith is our own, not a market segment.
- Builders *and* counselors — never "not consultants/advisors." Advisory value is named explicitly (Founder Call, Roadmap, Partner).
- No mechanism talk: agent orchestration never appears; outcomes and speed only.
- No self-undercutting of the prospect's work: their prototype is "a real start," never slop.
- Prices public and specific; the free call is the only unpriced rung and it must deliver real value, not a pitch.
- Ownership shelf: invisible in all public copy; never an upsell.
- Ad copy: third person, host-read cadence, original spine and close preserved.
