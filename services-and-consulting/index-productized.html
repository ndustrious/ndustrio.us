Questions

The honest answers.

How fast will I get my request?

Most single requests land in about 48 hours. Bigger features are broken into shippable pieces and delivered one after another, so you see steady progress the whole way — never a multi-week silence.

What does "one at a time" mean?

You can queue as many requests as you like; we actively build one at a time. It gets our full attention and ships fast, then we pull the next. Need two moving at once? That's The Double.

Is it really unlimited?

Unlimited requests in your queue and unlimited revisions, yes. We work them at the pace two senior builders can genuinely ship well — we'd rather tell you that plainly than promise magic.

Why do you cap the number of clients?

Because the promises on this page — 48-hour rhythm, senior attention on everything, counsel that knows your product — have a real ceiling, and we'd rather turn work away than quietly break them. The spot counter is live and truthful. If we're full, the waitlist is the honest answer, and we'll give you a straight estimate of the wait.

Can I pause?

Yes — and we mean it. Building in bursts, or waiting on your own users? Pause your subscription and stop paying; your queue and product wait for you. Resume in a click.

One honest caveat that comes with the roster cap: a paused subscription keeps your spot for [HOLD PERIOD: e.g. 60 days]. Pause longer and your spot may go to the waitlist — you'd rejoin at the front of the line. We'll always tell you before that happens.

Do I own what you build?

Completely. Your accounts, your code, your product, from the first day. No lock-in, no hostage infrastructure.

Do we have to meet?

Only if you want to. The work runs async on a shared board. We're happy to hop on a call when it actually helps — never as a tax on getting started.

Why a subscription instead of a quote per project?

Because most products aren't one job — they're a stream of them. A subscription means no re-quoting, no re-negotiating scope, no hourly meter. One price, work whenever you have it, pause when you don't.
